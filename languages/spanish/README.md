# spanish

Spanish inflection for **any** Spanish word, attested or not: noun plurals,
adjective agreement, and the whole conjugation (every simple tense, both
imperfect subjunctives, voseo, imperatives, participles), with a confidence for
every answer.

```rust
use spanish::*;

assert_eq!(noun("canción", Number::Plural), "canciones");
assert_eq!(noun("joven", Number::Plural), "jóvenes");
assert_eq!(adjective("feliz", Gender::Feminine, Number::Plural), "felices");
assert_eq!(verb("contar", Tense::Present, Person::First, Number::Singular), "cuento");
assert_eq!(verb("hacer", Tense::Preterite, Person::First, Number::Singular), "hice");
assert_eq!(verb("deshacer", Tense::Preterite, Person::Third, Number::Singular), "deshizo");
assert_eq!(vos("tener", Mood::Indicative), "tenés");
assert_eq!(participle("escribir", Gender::Feminine, Number::Plural), "escritas");
```

Written accents are part of the word: `papá` and `papa` are two words.

A citation gives principal parts the way a grammar lists an irregular verb:
first person singular present, first person singular preterite, participle
(`hacer, hago, hice, hecho`). Nouns take their gender as a marker (`mapa m`).

## How it works

This crate is Spanish inflection as a grammar, learned by
[flexion](https://github.com/gold-silver-copper/flexion) from the English Wiktionary's and the Spanish Wiktionary's
Spanish entries. Each part of speech is a module under `src/`:

- `paradigms.rs`: how words inflect, one table per paradigm, named after a word that
  follows it, with a pattern per form (`~а`: the stem, then `а`);
- `rules.rs`: which paradigm a word takes, as rules on its ending, each with how many of
  the dictionary's words with that ending follow each paradigm, and the words no rule
  gets right, listed;
- `irregular.rs`: the forms no paradigm gives.

A word the dictionary lists gets its own forms. Any other word gets the paradigm its
ending suggests, with a probability that says how often such a guess is right, and the
reason (the rule and its counts).

## The grammar

| | words | paradigms | rules | listed words | irregular forms |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| noun | 60268 | 21 | 202 | 687 | 438 |
| adjective | 24552 | 27 | 230 | 442 | 465 |
| verb | 17975 | 162 | 603 | 741 | 49010 |
| determiner | 60 | 12 | 7 | 18 | 0 |

*Words*: the dictionary's words the grammar is learned from. *Rules*: the endings that
choose a paradigm. *Listed words*: words no rule gets right, listed with their paradigm.
*Irregular forms*: forms no paradigm gives, listed.

## How well it does

A tenth of the words (by a hash of the word) was held out: the grammar scored here was
learned from the other nine tenths, and asked for every form the dictionary lists of
each held-out word. *Top-1*: its first answer is right; *top-3*: the right answer is among
its first three. *Calibration error*: how far its stated probabilities are, on average,
from how often it is right (0 is perfect).

| | held-out words | forms | top-1 | top-3 | calibration error |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| noun | 6082 | 12164 | **99.10%** | 99.88% | 0.002 |
| adjective | 2402 | 9619 | **98.84%** | 99.85% | 0.005 |
| verb | 931 | 67865 | **96.57%** | 99.38% | 0.016 |
| determiner | 4 | 16 | **87.50%** | 100.00% | 0.062 |
